About Me

I am a high school student with a passion for mathematics, economics, leadership, and creative expression. I enjoy exploring ideas from both analytical and artistic perspectives, believing that curiosity grows strongest when logic meets creativity.


Outside the classroom, I dedicate my time to music, dance, sports, storytelling, and community service. I have studied piano and ukulele for over a decade, trained in ballet and Latin dance for twelve years, competed in skateboarding for eleven years, and actively pursue rock climbing and sprinting. I have also spent seven years learning Spanish and love reading literature from different cultures.


Creativity has always been a major part of my life. I independently wrote and directed a full Harry Potter-themed school play, published a fiction novel, completed a twenty-chapter nonfiction manuscript exploring the historical roots of gender inequality, and continue sharing study advice through my social media page while tutoring IELTS learners online as a hobby.


Looking ahead, I hope to combine my interests in economics, mathematics, leadership, and public service to develop innovative solutions that create meaningful and lasting social impact.
